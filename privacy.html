Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Who we are

LedgeRays Systems ("LedgeRays", "we", "us") builds custom business systems, dashboards, websites and digital tools for businesses and individuals. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. You can reach us at ledgerays@outlook.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you use the contact form on this website, we receive your name, business name, email address, type of business, mobile number and your message. We also receive the information you include when you email us or message our Facebook page.

Public business contact information. To introduce our services, we may collect contact details that businesses publish openly, such as business name, public email address, phone number, website and Facebook page, from public directories and business websites.

Technical information. Our website host may automatically record basic technical data, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, to keep the site secure and running.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiries and provide our services
  • To prepare proposals and deliver projects you request
  • To send occasional, relevant information about our services to businesses
  • To keep our website and systems secure

You can stop receiving emails from us at any time by replying "stop" or emailing ledgerays@outlook.com. We will not contact you again.

How the contact form works

Messages sent through our contact form are delivered to our email inbox by FormSubmit, a form delivery service. FormSubmit processes the form data only to deliver it to us.

Use of AI tools

We use AI services to help sort messages and draft replies, which we review before anything is sent. Only business contact details and message content are processed for this purpose. We never use data from client systems with these tools.

Google user data

We use an internal tool that connects only to LedgeRays' own Google accounts to manage our records and email. It does not request or access Google account data belonging to anyone else.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Client systems

Systems we build for clients store their data in the client's own accounts, such as their own Google account. We access client data only with the client's permission and only as needed to build, fix or support their system.

Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as Google, Microsoft, Cloudflare, Telegram and FormSubmit, and only as needed for them to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required by law.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and contact information for as long as needed to respond to you, provide our services and meet legal obligations. Contacts who ask us to stop are kept only on a do-not-contact list so we never contact them again.

Security

We protect information with secure accounts, two-step verification and limited access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and similar laws, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it. Email ledgerays@outlook.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Our services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its effective date.
